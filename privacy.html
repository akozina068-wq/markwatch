Privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

What we collect

Your brand details. To watch your mark we store the brand name and the goods/services you tell us to watch. We match these against public trademark filings. Please don’t enter anything confidential beyond what you want monitored.

Payments. Payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email and the amount paid. We never see your card details.

Where the trademark data comes from

We monitor official, public registers: the UK IPO Trade Marks Journal and the EUIPO/TMview databases. We do not scrape private or consumer sites.

Why we collect it

To watch the registers for marks that may conflict with yours, alert you, give paying users access, send receipts and handle refunds. We don’t sell your data or share it for other companies’ marketing.

Where it’s stored

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Payment and subscription records are kept by Stripe for as long as the law requires.

Cookies and advertising

We use the Meta Pixel to measure whether our ads work. It only loads if you click “OK” on our cookie banner; click “No thanks” and it never loads. When it does load, it may set cookies and send Meta information about your visit, such as the page viewed and whether you subscribed. You can control ad tracking in your Facebook and Instagram ad settings and in your browser.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete the data we hold about you. Reply to any email we send you and we’ll act within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

About us

Markwatch is an independent trademark-monitoring and alert service. It is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. We tell you when a potentially conflicting mark is filed and when your deadline falls; a qualified attorney handles any opposition.

Refunds & cancellation

Markwatch is £24.99/month and you can cancel any time. If it isn’t useful, reply to your Stripe receipt within 7 days and we’ll refund you in full.
